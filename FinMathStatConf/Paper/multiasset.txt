In this talk we review the concepts of comonotonicity and
antimonotonicity between random variables in the sense of copula. We
then apply the ideas to solving the optimization problem of the
arbitrage bounds on multiasset options in the single period setting,
especially in the case of basket and the generalized spread options.
Accompanying with the bounds are the corresponding super and sub
hedging portfolios which are obtained by solving the dual problems.
These portfolios can be used to introduce a new measure of
codependence among the constituent underlying assets.
